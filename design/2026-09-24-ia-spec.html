Deliberately absent: an About section and a testimonials block. You have no testimonials yet, and an empty or thin version of either costs more credibility than leaving it out. The person is visible in the process section instead, where it answers a real question.

Navigation

LabelTargetWhy this word
Work#workShorter and less formal than "Portfolio," which reads like a student.

Process#processAnswers the real anxiety: what is this going to be like.

Four nouns and one verb, and the verb is styled as a button so the grammar stays parallel. Under the seven-item limit with room for one more.

04

Section specs

01

Hero

Problem named, category established, one action. Composed at rest, nothing animating in.

Out: “See the work” until there is work.

02

The three services

Three blocks, one sentence title each, two lines of body. This is the subhead expanded.

Each links to its matching demo once live.

03

The work

Three pieces. Large image, one line on what it had to do, link out to the live site.

Blocked. Structure defined now, filled as pieces land.

04

Made, not bought

The argument. Why a template can't be distinctive: thousands of people using it is its selling point.

Perceived quality only. No conversion claims.

05

How it works

Numbered steps with real durations. Where solo becomes a benefit: no handoff, no account manager.

Real weeks. Invented timelines get caught.

06

What I don't build

Checkout, accounts, dashboards, internal tools, apps. Stated plainly.

Highest-leverage section on the page.

07

Pricing

From-figures per service. Not a quote, a floor.

Removes the cheap enquiry before the call.

08

Start a project

Short form. What they're building, when it has to be live, budget band.

Date field does the qualifying.

05

Flows

Cold visitor, qualified

Hero → services → work → pricing → what I don't build → start

Proof before price. They need to want it before the number means anything.

Cold visitor, unqualified

Hero → services → what I don't build → leaves

This is a success. A dashboard enquiry that bounces here costs nothing; on a call it costs an hour.

Referral, already sold

Lands → start

Needs the CTA persistent in the nav so it is never more than one click away.

06

The one real blocker

Don't launch this page before demo 1 exists

The work section sits third, right where a visitor decides whether to keep reading, and it is the only section that proves the headline. A page that says your website is stock, next to no evidence that the author can do better, argues against itself.

Nothing stops the page being built now. The structure does not depend on the demos existing, only the launch does. Build the page and the helmet piece in parallel, fill the slot, then ship.
