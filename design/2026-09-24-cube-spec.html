Cube spec · weighted + S3 · step 6 · 24 Sep 2026

Weighted, resting corner on.

At the size it ships. Spin it in any direction and it carries the spin, bleeding it off slowly the way a model globe does. Rest your pointer on it and it slows faster, then settles corner on. Each face marks itself as you point at it, so a click is never a guess.

03

Input

Pointer

A press that moves under 7px and lasts under 320ms is a click, and a click kills any spin rather than starting one. Anything else is a drag, and the trailing click the browser fires afterwards is swallowed for 60ms. Clicks land on faces only; the space around the cube does nothing, because at a three-face pose there is no single face a background click could honestly mean.

Touch

Same thresholds. There is no hover, so a tap on a face opens it directly and a flick spins it exactly as a pointer does. Nothing needs releasing, because a coast ends by itself. touch-action:none is on the cube box only, never the section, so the page still scrolls.

Keyboard

Six faces are real buttons in DOM order, service then work, 01 through 03. Focus settles the cube to that face and draws the same mark. Enter or Space opens. Escape closes and focus returns to the face it came from.

Reduced motion

No loop runs at all. The cube is composed at a fixed corner pose showing three faces, drag and inertia are off, and clicking and tabbing work exactly as they do otherwise. It is a static object, not a slower one.

05

What it must not do

  • No bounce, no overshoot, no collision, nothing ballistic. It carries a spin and loses it to friction; it is never thrown and never hits anything.
  • Never exceed the ceiling, however hard it is dragged. The cap is not a tuning preference, it is the thing that makes the object read as having mass.
  • Never rotate on scroll. Scroll belongs to the page.
  • Never come to rest where no face can be clicked.
  • Never swallow a page scroll gesture on touch.
  • No entrance animation that starts the section empty. The cube renders composed.
  • No shadow, bevel, gloss or rounded corner. It is the same flat system as the rest of the page.
  • No second accent. Colour on the cube comes only from the three demos.

06

Still open

  • The overlay itself has not been designed. This spec only says a click opens one, and that opposite faces open the same one.
  • No demo exists, so the three colour faces are placeholders. The section can ship before they do, but the overlays cannot.
  • The layout architecture sheet still describes three equal service cells across twelve columns. It needs amending to the split.
