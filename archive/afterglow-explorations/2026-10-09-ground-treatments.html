Three ways the fall becomes the ground

The same sheet in each. The question is only where the gradient lives: behind the sheets, inside them, or beside them as a device. Everything else stays as it is.

Treatment A

The fall is behind the sheets

The gradient runs gold to violet down the whole deck and the sheets float on it as dark panels. The descent is visible between every sheet and in the margins, and nothing ever has to be read off a mid-ramp colour. Closest to what we already decided, and the safest for data.

Treatment B

Each sheet is a slice of the fall

Treatment C

The fall is a device, not the field

The ground is the deep end of the ramp rather than black, and the full fall runs as a band down the edge of every sheet. The brand is present on every page without competing with a table. Quietest of the three.
