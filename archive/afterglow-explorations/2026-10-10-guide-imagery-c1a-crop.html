C1a, three ways to stop cropping

Head and ground unchanged. The references are the same six per sheet in options one and three, four in option two. The source frames run 0.66 to 1.90 in aspect, mostly portrait with three landscape, which is why a six-across row turns each one into a slit.

1

Justified row, nothing cropped

Every frame keeps its native proportion. Heights match, widths vary, so the row reads as one band and no image loses a pixel.

Six references, zero crop. The landscape frames become wide and the portraits narrow, so the rhythm is irregular by design. Closest to how the photographs actually are.
