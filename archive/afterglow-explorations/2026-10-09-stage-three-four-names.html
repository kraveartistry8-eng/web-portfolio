Stages three and four, defined

Both have a locked hex, locked OKLCH and measured contrast. What neither has is a definition: the design system gives them the same one sentence, ground only, never a letterform, which is true, is negative, and is identical for two values that do completely different work.

Below: what the arithmetic already decided, then the one part that is still a choice. Stage one is named for an hour and stage two was named in a pass of its own. Three and four came verbatim out of the brief's prose and have never been through one.

The void is the fifth step

Measuring the fall against the ground rather than within itself turns up something that is not written down anywhere. The ramp steps 0.161, 0.158, 0.160 in OKLCH lightness. The gap from deep violet to the void is 0.167 — the same step again. The light does not stop at stage four and then sit on a canvas. It keeps deepening, one more time, into the ground.

The part that is still a choice

Three families. Each is shown in the sequence it would join, because a name only has to survive being read next to the other three. Every candidate draws at least one word from the list in voice-tone.md, so stage_named passes either way — that check only tests stage two today and should be widened to all four whichever you pick.

A

Bloom / Hold

One word each, the way Corridor was proposed for stage two.

Bloom is the brief's own verb: the deepening scent lifts and blooms. It names the peak rather than the pigment. Hold is the other half of the same sentence, the heat holding steady to close, and it is what the brand's last line claims: the night ends but its afterglow does not. Both are in the vocabulary, so stage_named passes.
