Three ways to be more than one page

The same four pages of the guideline, paged three ways. The fall is unchanged: four stages, flat chroma at 0.110, lightness falling 0.781 to 0.301, and the text flipping from void to light at the halfway point. What changes is where the document breaks and what the break does to the descent.

Iteration 1

Snap pages, one fall

The fall is one gradient and every page is a window onto it. Scroll snaps, so you land on a composition instead of between two. Page 01 ends at #C38051 and page 02 begins at the same value, which is why the seam does not read as a seam.

Three stages are not on the ramp, so they cannot carry text. The later stages are specified in the column to the right, which is the fall in the rule. The failing numbers are printed because they are the reason for the restriction.

Sheets on the void

Iteration 3

Separate documents

Four files, one per stage, each with the slice of the fall it occupies. The navigation names where you are going by its lightness, so the rule is still visible at the moment you move. The cost is that nobody ever sees the whole descent at once.
