Stage two, six names in the corridor direction

The ramp is settled. Ramp E was picked on 9 October: chroma flat at 0.110, lightness falling in even steps, the arc running furthest into the cool end. Stage two is the last null in the colour system.

What the corridor direction means here. The other three stages are named for light or for colour. The corridor names the passage instead, the bit between two rooms where nothing is being shown. Every candidate below keeps that idea and draws at least one word from the list in voice-tone.md, which is not a style preference: the stage_named check reads that list from the file and fails a name built from anything else.

Where this came from. Pass 2 offered three names alongside the ramps. Aaron picked ramp E and said the corridor was the right direction for the name but wanted more of them. This page explores only that direction; it does not revisit the ramp.

What came next. After gold was picked on 9 October, which closes the colour system. Type is the one remaining visual pick.

The warm room

Draws on the brief's line that each room runs warmer than the last. That line was corrected on 7 Oct: it is about air temperature for scent diffusion and was scoped event-only, and the stages are explicitly not required to warm in hue. Naming a stage for it would quietly put the correction back.
