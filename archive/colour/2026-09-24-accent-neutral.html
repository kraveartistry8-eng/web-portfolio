Phase 0 · accent, resolved

The container stops having a colour

2026 / 09 / 24 · neutrals and type unchanged · no tinted fills

Every hue is category-locked, so no accent can serve all three demos. The fix is to stop asking it to. The accent becomes a near-black with a trace of hue, the tinted fill disappears, and the mark inverts to light-on-dark. All colour in the portfolio now comes from the work.
