Accent · locked

Cyan accent, two steps, action only.

Your cyan is a dark-mode color: 12.48:1 on the dark ground, 1.31:1 on the light one. It needs a darker step of the same hue to survive light mode. Used for links, the one highlighted word in a headline, and nothing else. If it appears anywhere decorative, it stops reading as action.

Cyan in light mode · specimens · approved uses

Approved: everywhere it is a surface. Never as ink.

#90e0ef against the light ground is 1.31:1. Ink on a cyan fill is 12.16:1. So the color is not unusable in light mode, it just has to be the thing behind the type instead of the type itself.
