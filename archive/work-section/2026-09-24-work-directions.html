B

Three plates

Keeps the site's existing card motif — white surfaces separated by a two-pixel hairline of ground — and gets its hierarchy purely from scale. The safest direction, and the one that looks most like the rest of the page.

Empty slot

Weakest here. An empty card is a visible hole, and a card with no image looks like a card that failed to load.

Surface

Reintroduces the white plate, so the page regains structure below the fold.

Risk

C

The index

No images at rest. Three rows of type — number, name, category, link — with a large preview that appears only on hover or focus. The work is a list you consult rather than a gallery you browse.

Empty slot

Strongest here. A row is a row. “Building now” is a status, not a gap, and the layout never changes shape.

Surface

Stays on bare ground, but the preview panel brings a large surface in on demand.

Risk

Hover is not available on touch. Phone needs the preview always visible under each row, which changes the idea at small sizes.

D

The colour enters

Each piece is a full-bleed band carrying its own demo's palette. The container is deliberately colourless so that this section can be loud — it is the one place the neutral decision pays off visibly.
