Spin it and let go.

All four carry the spin you give them and bleed it off with friction. The difference is what they are mounted on and where they end up. Flick each one hard, then flick it gently, and watch the two numbers in the corner: how fast it is turning, and what state it is in.

Four ways to have weight

Resting your pointer on a spinning cube adds drag, the way a finger on a globe does. Faces still mark themselves on hover and still open on click, at any speed.
