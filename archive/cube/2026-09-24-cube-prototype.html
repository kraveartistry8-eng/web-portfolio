Drag it. Then click a face.

Type is printed on the faces and rolls with them. Put the pointer on the cube and it eases to a stop with the nearest face square on, which is the only moment anyone needs the words level. Drag to kick it, let go and it keeps the spin, then settles back to a drift. Click or tab to a face to open it.
