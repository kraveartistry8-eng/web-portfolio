Direction A · four solids · 24 Sep 2026

The solid, four ways.

All four tumble on three axes at once, continuously. Every face counter-rotates its own type so the words stay upright at every angle. Cards are identical in all four, so the only thing changing is the cube. Hover a cube to freeze it.

A1Bare No borders anywhere. White meets colour edge to edge, so it reads as one solid object rather than six panels. Even drift on all three axes.
A2Ruled Every face carries an inset 1px rule, the page’s one repeated gesture applied to the object. Near-flat perspective, slowest tumble. Reads editorial rather than toy. The rule belongs to the cube, so it rolls; only the type stays upright.
A3Big Scaled up on a deep perspective so it dominates its half. Service faces drop to a single mono numeral, because the titles already sit in the cards to the left and saying them twice was redundant.
A4Grounded A contact shadow puts it on a surface instead of in space, and it bobs as it turns. The only one with apparent weight, which is what makes a kick believable.
