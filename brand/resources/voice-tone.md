# Afterglow voice and tone

## The core rule: withhold, do not withhold information

Afterglow describes what happens to you, not what the product contains. The bottle, the
notes list and the price are not hidden, they simply never lead. Copy earns its way to them.

## Voice

**Primary.** Unhurried and physical. It names heat, light, breath and skin. It describes a
sequence, because the product is a sequence.

**Secondary.** Certain without pushing. The brand does not argue for itself. It assumes you
will understand it if you are shown it in the right order.

## Words that belong to Afterglow

sundown, last light, deepen, bloom, hold, note, breath, skin, room, corridor, violet, rose,
gold, warm, after

## Words that do not belong to Afterglow

luxury, premium, elevated, indulge, treat yourself, must-have, bestselling, limited time,
shop now, unlock, discover (as a CTA), game-changing

## Grammar

- Complete sentences. No sentence fragments used as atmosphere.
- No exclamation marks.
- No em-dashes. Periods and commas carry the pauses.
- Never spell the brand in caps in body copy. `AFTERGLOW` is a wordmark, `Afterglow` is a word.

## Tone by context

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Home, above the fold | Sets the hour. Says almost nothing about the product |
| Collection | The four stages in order. Never reads as four products |
| Product | The sequence, then the detail, then the bottle, in that order |
| Cart and checkout | Plain and fast. The atmosphere stops here on purpose |
| Transactional email | Useful first. One line of voice at most |

## What it leaves behind

The brand's own line, from the source material: "Guests carry the scent out on their skin
and in their memory. It finds them in quiet moments for weeks." And the name's logic: the
night ends but its afterglow does not.
