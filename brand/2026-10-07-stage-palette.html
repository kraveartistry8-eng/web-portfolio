Flat chroma, three ways through the arc

Chroma is now held flat at 0.110 across all four stages, which is where ramp C started and the paleness you picked. Lightness still falls in even steps of 0.16. Stage 1 is held at the same hue in all three, so every one of them opens on exactly the gold you chose, #D8B260, and the only thing moving is where the arc travels after it.

What flat and pale costs, stated plainly. At 0.110 the middle of the ramp goes dusty. Rose is no longer the clear crimson it was at 0.170; it lands nearer a dried brick or a mauve depending on the arc. That is not a fault in any of these three, it is what holding chroma down does to the stages that were carrying the most of it. If the rose below reads too drab, the fix is chroma, not hue, and we go back a step.

Every ratio on this page is computed from the hex beside it. Stage 1 has to clear 4.5:1 on the void to be usable as text and stage 2 has to clear 3:1 for large text. Stages 3 and 4 cannot clear either and are not meant to; they are grounds, and the number is printed so the restriction has a reason attached to it.
