Afterglow · working artifact · 7 October 2026

Three candidate ramps for the four stages

All three share one lightness schedule, falling in even steps of 0.16 in OKLCH, and one hue schedule rotating a single direction along the sundown arc. Chroma shaping is the only variable, so what you are comparing is how saturated the middle of a sunset should be and whether violet holds its colour as it darkens.

Every ratio on this page is computed from the hex beside it. Stage 1 has to clear 4.5:1 on the void to be usable as text and stage 2 has to clear 3:1 for large text. Stages 3 and 4 cannot clear either and are not meant to; they are grounds, and the number is printed so the restriction has a reason attached to it.
