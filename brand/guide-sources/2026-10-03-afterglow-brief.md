# Afterglow brand brief

> **Afterglow is one fragrance told in four stages, from the gold of last light
> to deep violet, and you do not see the bottle until the end.**

Fictional brand for the Phase 1 build. Source material: `Frequency Alignment/Tunsi/tracker/config/event.config.ts`,
written for the launch-night production tracker. Everything below is pulled from there.

---

## What it is

A signature fragrance. One scent, not a range. Its launch is a night: 17 October 2026,
a Brooklyn venue, doors at 7:00 PM sharp.

## The central idea

Editors and press meet Afterglow by **walking through it**. Four rooms trace the sky after
sundown, from the gold of last light through rose to deep violet. Each room is a stage of
the scent. Nobody sees the bottle until the end.

## The scent architecture

Top note to base, spread across the four rooms. A neutral reset corridor between every room
clears the nose for the next. Diffusion is tuned to each room's heat so the scent blooms on cue.

Guests meet the scent **as a journey, not a cloud**.

## Two rules that are really art direction

1. **The light only ever deepens, never brightens.** Gold in the first room, violet in the
   last, holding all night.
2. **Each room runs warmer than the last**, so the deepening scent lifts and blooms, and the
   heat holds steady to close.

## The brand rule, which is the storefront brief

> "The brand is unmistakable in every corner **without a logo in sight**."
> "The brand is felt in the world, never stamped on it."

This is the hardest and most useful constraint in the whole document. It rules out the
default move of putting a wordmark on everything and calling it art direction.

## What it leaves behind

> "Guests carry the scent out on their skin and in their memory. It finds them in quiet
> moments for weeks, and every time, they want to be back in that room."

And the name's own logic: *"The night ends but its afterglow does not."*

## Colour

**Transfers from the source file.** The void ground and its surface steps, plus the neutrals.

| Token | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| ground | `#080808` | A dark ground is required by the deepening-light rule, not borrowed |
| surface-deep | `#141414` | |
| surface | `#2B2B2B` | |
| text | `#EEECEA` | |
| muted | `#C0C0C0` | |
| border | `rgba(192,192,192,0.22)` | hairline |

**Does not transfer.** `#39FF14` and `#FF00BD`. The source file states their job: neon green
means "system online", magenta is the trouble accent for blocked and at-risk states, always
paired with an icon. Dashboard status colours. Afterglow has no equivalent job for them.

**Still to define, in week 2.** Gold of last light, rose, deep violet. Four stages, only ever
deepening. These exist as prose and have no hex values yet. They are exactly what Shopify's
`color_palette` setting type is for, which replaced colour schemes in June 2026 and is a
week 2 task. The brief and the platform feature line up without either being bent.

## Also undefined, and deliberately

- The type scale, grid and spacing. Week 2.
- What the bottle looks like. It is revealed last, so it can be designed last.
- The product itself: size, concentration, price, whether there is a refill.
