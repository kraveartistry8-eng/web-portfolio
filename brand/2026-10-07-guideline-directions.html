Three ways the document could be built

Each panel below is a spread from the document itself, at working scale, with real copy and measured numbers. The commentary sits outside the spread, never inside it. Stage colours are provisional and chosen only so the structures can be judged; the ramp is a separate decision.

Direction A

The Descent

The light only ever deepens.

Body copy may begin here4.5:1

Above this line the ground is too light to carry body copy, so only display sizes are set. Below it, the fall has deepened far enough that running text clears the floor and holds for the rest of the document. The line is not decoration. It is the measurement, drawn where it falls.

Nothing after this point returns to a lighter value. The document has one direction, and a reader always knows how far through it they are by how dark the page has become.

The page is the brand

The ground deepens continuously across the whole document. Position is read as colour, and the ramp doubles as the folio.

Neither reference does this. Frame.io holds a fixed near-black and paginates; Uber lets photography carry the mood over white chrome.

The hard problem is text surviving the fall, so the spread shows it solved rather than claimed: a measured crossover line, display type above it, body copy only below.

Cost. Every section’s type colour depends on its scroll position, which is the most fragile thing here.

The structure quotes the brief

Four chapters, one per stage, with a bare void corridor between each. Rules live in the corridors, demonstrations live in the rooms.

The corridor is a full band, not a gutter, so the reset is felt rather than noticed. On a phone the sequence stays vertical and in order, which is the one thing this direction cannot afford to lose.

Cost. The quote only lands if the reader knows the launch night, and the event rules were scoped out of the site.

The document proves itself

A foundry specimen rather than a brand book. Each field is shown at working scale carrying its own hex, OKLCH triple and both measured ratios, set on the colour being specified.

The two ends of the ramp sit side by side so the crossover is visible in one glance: gold is a mark on the void, violet is a ground that carries text. Same rule, opposite directions.

Cost. Densest of the three and the most likely to read as technical rather than as the brand.
