JSON-LD, Schema, and the AIO claim

The one row on the list that testing contradicts.

Structured data is cheap, harmless and worth shipping. The claim that it drives AI discovery is contested by 2026 controlled testing, and the lift figures circulating alongside it name no sample and no method.

01

What it actually is

Structured data is machine-readable markup describing what a page is about, usually JSON-LD in a script tag using Schema.org vocabulary. On a storefront that means product markup with price, availability and reviews, which is what produces rich results in Google.

"AIO" is a coinage, not a standard. It is used to mean optimising for AI answer engines, alongside the similar acronyms AEO and GEO. None of them names a specification.

02

Does the claim hold

The Schema.org half is real and uncontroversial. The "drives zero-click AI discovery and semantic search visibility" half does not survive the available testing.

A February 2026 controlled experiment found that ChatGPT and Perplexity tokenise JSON-LD as raw text rather than parsing it semantically. A separate multi-engine experiment across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Mode found every system extracted visible HTML content, while hidden structured data in script tags, Microdata attributes and RDFa annotations was not processed at the point of citation selection.

Against that sit vendor claims: 2.5x higher chance of appearing in AI answers, 40% more AI Overview appearances, 3.2x more citations. None names a sample size or a method. That asymmetry is the finding.

03

The devil's advocate

  1. The practical instruction inverts

    If engines read visible content, then the way to be cited is to put the answer on the page in text a human can read, not to hide it in markup. That is a content and layout decision, which is your discipline, rather than a technical one.

  2. Ship it anyway, for a different reason

    Structured data still drives Google rich results, and the most balanced reading found is that schema helps AI citation indirectly through Google's own pipeline rather than directly. It costs half a day. The error is not doing it, the error is selling it.

  3. This is where a competitor will out-promise you

    Somebody will pitch your prospect on AI visibility with a 3.2x number attached. You will not, because you cannot source it. Losing that deal is the cost of the positioning in section 03, and it is the correct trade, but it is worth knowing the moment will come.
