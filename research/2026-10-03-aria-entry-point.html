01

Executive summary

The question was the lowest entry point that still pays on the way to a design-led studio. The research moved the answer. I had put a paid audit at the bottom rung. The market data says the audit is the wrong bottom rung, and that a performance fix sold on a measured before and after is a better one at three times the price.

Three findings carry the decision.

  • Anything describable as “audit” or “theme customization” is already sold at $45 to $300. This is observable, not estimated. Shopify’s own Partners directory lists theme customization starting at $49, $50, $85, $100, $150, $200, $249. You cannot win that category and you should not enter it.
  • The productized middle is visible and priced. Storetasker publishes live starting prices: Landing Page Customization $400, Theme Edits $475, New PDP Layout $2,000 over two weeks, Site Speed Optimization $2,000 over one month. These are the real rungs, and two of them map onto weeks already in your curriculum.
  • The speed fix needs no case study. Every other entry offer requires proof you will not have until mid-November. A performance engagement proves itself inside the engagement, because the deliverable is a number the client can check in the same tool you used.

What changed from the first answer

I said $750 for thinking, $2,000 for building. The building floor survived contact with the data and is now market-confirmed at $2,000. The $750 audit did not survive. Audits clear anywhere from $45 to $5,000 with nothing defensible in the middle, and $750 lands exactly in the dead zone: too expensive to buy on impulse, too cheap to read as expertise.

The shape of it: from 9 October to mid-November the only thing you can honestly sell is the teardown, and only to people who already know your name. From roughly 14 November two real offers open at once, and both of them are weeks you were already going to do. That is the whole sequencing answer.

Your assumed day rate is half right

The roadmap assumes agency subcontract at $900 to $1,600 a day. Across every source found, freelance Shopify developers run $30 to $285 an hour, with mid-level clustering near $78 and senior near $133. Eight hours at $900 is $112 an hour, which sits at the top of the freelance band and is defensible for a specialist with proof. Eight hours at $1,600 is $200 an hour, which is above every freelance figure found and inside boutique-agency territory at $90 to $175. Plan on $900. Treat $1,600 as a ceiling you may never touch as a subcontractor.

Confidence 6/10. Every rate source here is vendor tier, meaning a company publishing numbers about its own users or its own hiring. No independent survey of Shopify subcontract rates was found.

03

The devil’s advocate

  1. The case against the audit is stronger than the case for it

    A great deal of writing argues that cheap front-end offers attract buyers who will never purchase the expensive thing. All of it that I found is folklore tier: no study, no sample, and almost every piece written by someone selling high-ticket coaching. Discount the argument.

    The observable pricing points the same way without needing the argument. The audit’s problem is not that it is cheap. Its problem is that at a glance it is indistinguishable from a $45 Fiverr gig, because the deliverable is a document and nobody can see a document before they buy it. A rebuilt page can be looked at. That is the whole difference.

  2. So the teardown is not a product. It is a conversation with an invoice attached.

    Which changes where it belongs. It works sold to someone who already knows your name and is already half-persuaded, so it goes to the warm 30 to 50 and nowhere else. Sold to a stranger it competes on price and loses. Keep it off the cold list entirely, and keep it at $500 flat so it reads as a deliberate first step rather than a quote.

  3. The survey says almost nobody charges what your roadmap charges

    Of 208 web designers surveyed in 2025, 57% priced projects between $2,500 and $9,999, and only 2.4% exceeded $10,000 a project. 66% earned under $50,000 for the year. Your $15k to $40k band sits in the top two percent of that distribution.

    Two readings and both are true. The sample is self-selected, recruited from one academy’s own audience, 60% US, and its authors call it directional rather than representative. It is also almost certainly measuring a mostly-small-business, mostly-not-Shopify population, and the Shopify-specific figures put mid-tier agency projects at $15,000 to $35,000. The band holds. What the survey should do is kill any assumption that this is a crowded, obvious move. Almost nobody is up there, which is the opportunity and the reason it will feel lonely and slow.

  4. The paid-discovery evidence is weaker than it sounds

    The same survey reports that only 15% charge for discovery upfront, and that those who do are twice as likely to land $5,000-plus projects. That is correlational, n=208, self-reported. It cannot carry a causal sentence. The likeliest explanation is that charging for discovery is a marker of the positioning and nerve that also produce bigger projects, not a cause of them. Use the direction. Do not quote the figure, and do not expect charging $500 to raise your prices by itself.

  5. The finding I underweighted the first time

    Site Speed Optimization, $2,000, one month, live on a marketplace of vetted Shopify freelancers. Core Web Vitals and a sub-200ms INP are already week 6 of your plan. This offer is sellable with no portfolio, because the proof is generated during delivery and the client can verify it in PageSpeed Insights themselves. Your own document already makes this argument: a number the client can check is worth more than an adjective. The research says that number is also the easiest thing you own to sell first.

04

The product route, and why it is parked

A theme sold on the Shopify Theme Store is the only entry point here that pays without a client, and on paper it is the best long-term fit for a design-led studio of one. The mechanics, from shopify.dev directly: no fee to submit, a 15% revenue share so you keep 85%, plus a 2.9% processing fee charged separately since 1 August 2021. You need a Partner Dashboard account and a demo store.

Three things disqualify it as the first paid thing. There is no published review timeline anywhere in the documentation. Once the review team starts, you cannot touch the theme until they finish. And themes are rejected on design sophistication and uniqueness, which is a subjective bar you cannot engineer against in advance.

Insufficient data available for Theme Store earnings

No source found, primary or otherwise, for what a published theme actually earns or for the distribution of earnings across themes. Shopify publishes the revenue share and not the revenue. Anyone quoting you a figure for this is guessing. Do not plan around it.

Park it. Revisit it in April, once there is a real storefront behind you and the question is how to earn while asleep rather than how to earn at all.

06

The verdict and the test

Lead with the performance fix at $2,000. Use the $500 teardown only on people who already know you.

The lowest entry point that still builds toward a design-led studio is not the cheapest thing you can sell. It is the cheapest thing you can sell whose proof is generated during delivery. That is the performance engagement, and it is $2,000, not $750.

Everything below $1,500 in this market is a race against agencies listing from $49 on Shopify’s own directory. You will not win it, and every weekend spent in it is a weekend taken out of the twenty-seven.

The test, and the date it resolves.

  • Batch: the warm 30 to 50, across the Friday sends from 9 Oct. One offer in the email, the $500 teardown, with a named deliverable and a one-week clock.
  • Window: closes 13 Nov, which is the send before the storefront is finished.
  • Deciding metric: paid teardowns, not replies. Replies are not conversations and conversations are not invoices.
  • Kill criteria: zero paid teardowns by 13 Nov means the offer is wrong, not the volume. Do not send more. Switch on 14 Nov to leading with the performance fix at a flat $2,000, which is the week the number becomes yours to show.
  • Floor rule: nothing free, nothing open-ended, half up front, and no engagement under $500 for any reason including the first one.
