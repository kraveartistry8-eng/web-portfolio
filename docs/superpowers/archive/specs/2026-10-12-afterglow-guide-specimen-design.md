## 1. Overview

Every sheet shows one thing, centred on its ground, with a short caption set like a museum label. Everything a reader looks up rather than looks at goes to four reference sheets at the back. Nothing is deleted:
- readings (hex, OKLCH, CMYK, contrast)
- tables
- rules lists
- tiles
- production notes

The guide keeps:
- its grounds falling from gold to black, sheet by sheet
- the hours
- its two typefaces
- the ember
- every one of its photographs

It gains one new asset: the mark.

The guide stops reading as a crowded document and starts reading as a set of specimens. One thing per page, and it is clear what to look at first.

## 2. The mark

Final geometry, approved by Aaron. It is stored as `brand/mark/afterglow-mark-light.svg` (#EEECEA) and `afterglow-mark-dark.svg` (#080808). These files are the source of truth, and the mark is never redrawn by eye.

**The parts.** Three equilateral triangles of the same side, S:
- **The bottle.** It points up, in the middle.
- **The A.** It points down, on the left, with its inner side parallel to the bottle's left slope.
- **The G.** It points down, on the right, mirroring the A.
- **The cap.** A rectangle centred over the bottle's peak.

**Spacing.** Every element stands apart from the next by the same gap: 0.16 S, measured square to the slope. The cap sits 0.26 S above the peak. Its width is 0.23 S and its height 0.27 S.

**Line work only.** One stroke weight throughout, 0.04 S, with mitred corners. There are no fills.

**The A** is its triangle read on its side, with its peak at the triangle's left corner.
- Its legs are the top edge and the outer side.
- The side facing the bottle is left open.
- Each foot carries a serif that runs 0.31 of the side along the open side, inward.

**The G.**
- Its strokes are the top edge, the inner side and the point.
- The outer side rises 0.42 of the way from the point, then turns in level towards the middle.
- The top edge's outer end carries a serif that runs 0.2 of the side down the outer side.
- The G's opening, 0.38 of the side, equals the gap between the A's two serifs.

**Sizes.** The mark is used at 48 px wide or larger on screen, which is where the A and G still read. The print minimum is set at the first print proof and written back here.

**Colour.** #EEECEA on dark grounds and #080808 on gold, never in a stage colour.

**Clear space.** At least the cap's height on every side.

The guide's line "There is no drawn logotype" becomes "The mark is three triangles: the bottle, the A and the G. The wordmark is Instrument Serif, widened to 150%."

## 3. The sheet grammar

Every non-reference sheet follows the same grammar.

**The running head** is unchanged: the section name, the hour and the page number at 11 px.

**One object**, centred in the sheet both ways. The object is one of these:
- the mark
- the wordmark
- a swatch row
- a type specimen
- a photograph
- a five-photograph strip
- a product

**One caption** under the object in Instrument Serif 18 px, at most two lines, never longer than 34 characters to the line.

**One tag** at the bottom left in Space Grotesk 11 px capitals, at most two short lines.

**At most three type sizes** on a sheet, the running head included. Hours count as part of the object.

Grounds are unchanged: each sheet keeps its slice of the fall, and no sheet is lighter than the one before it. The ember is unchanged. Nothing is placed, sized or cleared for it; the open ground is the result of having one object per sheet.

## 5. Typography

- **Objects:** Instrument Serif 400. The wordmark is widened to 150%. The hours keep −0.03 em tracking and are never widened.
- **Captions:** Instrument Serif 400, 18 px, line height 1.35.
- **Tags and running head:** Space Grotesk 400, 11 px, 0.12 to 0.14 em tracking, capitals.
- **Reference text:** Space Grotesk 400, 11 px, line height 1.6. Headings are Instrument Serif at 16 to 20 px.

## 6. Responsive notes

The breakpoints are the guide's existing ones: 576, 900 and 992 px.

**Desktop, above 992 px (1440 and 1024)**
- Sheets stay 820 px tall at 1440.
- Objects are centred both ways. Their sizes scale with the viewport below 1440, with the 1440 sizes as the maximum.
- At 1024 the colour-page strip is about 690 px wide, still on one row.

**Tablet, 577 to 992 px (768)**
- Sheets take their natural height, at least the stable small-viewport height.
- The strip wraps:
  - the lead frame is centred on its own row at 260 × 325
  - the four others sit in one row beneath it at 140 × 175
- The swatch row stays four across.
- On page 14 the label, poster and card stay on one row, scaled to 80%.
- Reference sheets drop to two columns.

**Phone, 576 px and below (390)**
- Sixteen-pixel gutters, one column, objects centred.
- The strip: the lead frame runs the full width less the gutters at 4:5, and the other four sit in a two-by-two grid beneath it.
- The swatches go two by two.
- The fall band runs the full width less the gutters, with its hours wrapping beneath on two lines.
- Captions stay 18 px and the cover wordmark becomes 64 px. The mark goes to 180 px wide on the cover and is never below 48 px anywhere.
- Page 14 stacks: the label first, then the poster and card side by side.
- Reference sheets go to one column, with the lead element first.

**Everywhere**
- Nothing scrolls sideways.
- Every numeral and the mark pass the fonts-blocked offline check that guards the hours today.
- The ember is unchanged.

## 7. What it must not do

- **Images:**
  - no images outside the guide's current set, and no board images
  - each image used once (the allowed repeat f3MgZ_1 stays only where it already is)
  - no imagery of Black women with short or shaved hair
- **Nothing deleted.** Every reading, table, rule, tile and note moves to the reference sheets, word for word.
- **The mark:**
  - no fills
  - never redrawn, distorted, recoloured into a stage colour, or used below 48 px wide
  - never placed on a photograph without clear space
- **Ground:** no sheet lighter than the sheet before it.
- **The hours:** never changed, reordered or animated.
- **Over photographs:** no scrim, shadow or blend.
- **The ember:** no space composed for it, and no change to its behaviour.
- **No borrowed grammar:** no three-column code / name / count label strip.
- **Shopify:** no change to the Shopify theme.
