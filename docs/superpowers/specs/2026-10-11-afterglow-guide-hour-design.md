# Afterglow guide: The hour

Status: draft for Aaron's approval (feature-design Step 6). Picked 11 Oct 2026 from the Step 3 storyboard, direction C, as shown: the hours alone, without Direction B's climbing names, with the readings kept on each sheet.

Storyboard: `.superpowers/brainstorm/2217-1791771260/C-hour.html`
Guide: `brand/2026-10-10-density-b1-column.html`, generated by `tools/density_b_family.py`
Reference held to: Bob Beauté by Kurppa Hosk, pinned at `references/ember/references/2026-10-11-bob-beaute-kurppa-hosk/`

## 1. Overview

The guide becomes one evening. Each of the four stages is an hour: Gold of last light is 19:12, After gold 19:40, Rose 20:15, Deep violet 21:30, and black is "dark". The hours are set as huge Instrument Serif numerals on the sheets that are about a stage. Every other sheet carries its own time in the running head. Because the sheet grounds already step down the fall from gold to black, those times run forwards through the document, from 19:12 on the cover to dark at the end.

What it communicates: the palette is not a set of swatches, it is a description of light at a particular hour (the art direction's own words, page 07). The clock is that idea made literal, and no other fragrance guide has it.

## 2. The hours

Four fixed hours, one per stage, never changed and never reordered:

## 3. Sheet by sheet

Grounds do not change. The one rule ("never lighter than the one before it") forbids the storyboard's flat gold ground on page 08 after the rose sheets, so every sheet keeps its place in the fall. The ember reads those grounds too.

**Running head, all sheets except the cover.** The head reads, left to right: the sheet's name, then its time, then its page number. The time is the same size and weight as the page number, in Space Grotesk at the caption size (11 px, tracked 0.14 em, upper case). The page number stays, so nothing in the guide becomes harder to find.

**01 Cover.** As storyboarded:
- #080808 ground, with the cover photograph (ZY6XxOv) as a full-height panel on the right, 38% of the width. It is cropped from the top, at 107% scale.
- 19:12 is set at 430 px, low left; it crosses onto the photograph.
- One note, top left: "One fragrance in four stages, each an hour of one evening, from 19:12 until it is dark."
- The wordmark stays in the running head ("Afterglow · brand guide", "October 2026").

**03 The four stages.** Four columns of flat stage colour, weighted 1.6 : 1 : 0.8 : 0.6, so each later hour gets less room. Each column carries:
- its hour, at 190, 120, 90 and 64 px
- its stage name, at 22 px Instrument Serif
- its readings beneath: hex, OKLCH, CMYK and every contrast pair. These are the readings the current sheet carries, in the same order; none are dropped.

**04 The fall.** The gradient bar keeps its percentages. Under each stop it adds that stop's hour (19:12, 19:40, 20:15, 21:30, dark), at the reading size. The magenta sliver at 100% has no hour.

**07 Art direction.** The opening rule gains the window: "Images are made between 19:12 and 21:30, or lit to look as though they were." Nothing else on the sheet changes.

**08 to 11, the imagery spreads.** Each spread has three parts:
- **The panel.** The lead frame is a full-height panel on the right, 38% wide. The leads are wRU1tGM, tLwiMVf, gE_ypMW and 7BF3FrZ.
- **The numeral.** The stage's hour is set at 520 px, low left, crossing onto the panel.
- **The rest.** The stage name, hex, L value and the one-line description sit top left, as a note at 12 px.

The spreads also carry the four supporting frames, and the storyboard does not show where they go. They are not dropped: they sit in one row across the left field, below the running head, at equal height. That placement has not been seen yet, so it is the first thing built and shown (see §8). The image set stays exactly the guide's current twenty frames, each used once.

**02, 05, 06, 12, 13, 14, 15.** Their layouts are unchanged; they carry the hour only in the running head. One exception: on 05 Two faces, one scale, the display specimen sets "19:12" beside "Afterglow". This shows the numerals are part of the type system rather than a decoration added on top.

## 4. Typography

- **Hour numerals:** Instrument Serif 400, line height 0.8, tracking −0.03 em, with lining figures and the colon from the face itself.
  - Sizes at 1440: 430 px on the cover, 520 px on the spreads, and 190, 120, 90 and 64 px on the colour sheet.
  - The numerals do not get the wordmark's 150% widening. That stays the wordmark's alone.
- **Notes:** Space Grotesk 400, 12 px, line height 1.55, at most 30 characters to the line.
- **Running head:** Space Grotesk 400, 11 px, tracking 0.14 em, upper case.
- **Everything else** keeps the existing type scale (1.25 ratio).

## 5. Colour and visual details

- **Numeral colour:** the sheet's existing ink, #EEECEA on dark grounds and #080808 on light ones.
  - On the colour sheet, each column's ink is the one its contrast reading already passes: #080808 on the gold and after-gold columns, #EEECEA on the rose and violet ones.
- **Where a numeral crosses the photograph,** it keeps its ink colour.
  - No scrim, shadow, blend mode or outline. The critic faulted Direction A's darkening as a UI scrim.
  - The crossing part must still pass 3:1 against the photograph behind it, as large text. If a lead frame fails that, its crop moves; the numeral does not get treated.
- **No new colours.** The hours add no hex values to the guide.

## 6. The ember

- The hour numerals are lit text: they join the ember's text selectors, and catch its light on the side facing it, like all other type.
- The running-head time is lit like the rest of the head.
- Nothing about the ember's timing, colour or behaviour changes.

## 7. Responsive notes

Breakpoints follow the guide's existing ones (576, 900 and 992 px), not new ones. The sheets are already fluid.

**Above 992 px (desktop, including 1440 and 1024):**
- Layouts are as described. The photograph panel stays at 38% of the sheet width and full sheet height.
- Numerals scale with the viewport, not as fixed pixels: the 1440 sizes are the maximum, and they shrink in proportion below 1440.
  - At 1024 the spread numeral is about 370 px and the cover numeral about 305 px. Both still cross onto the panel.

**577 to 992 px (tablet, including 768):**
- The panel stays on the right at 38%, full height.
- Numerals shrink so the widest hour ("20:15", "21:30") covers no more than 85% of the sheet width. That puts the spread numeral at about 280 px at 768.
- The four supporting frames on the spreads go from one row of four to two rows of two.
- The colour sheet keeps its four weighted columns. Each column's readings stack, with no column narrower than 120 px, so the 0.6 column is the binding one.

**576 px and below (phone, including 390):**
- The panel moves on top, full width, at 4:5, using the same crop. The guide's images hold at 2× here: 780 px wide is under their 2000 px.
- The numeral sits on the ground directly under the panel, overlapping its foot by a quarter of the numeral's height. It is sized so the widest hour fills the width inside the 16 px gutters, about 150 px at 390.
- The note and readings follow below in a single column.
- The supporting frames become a two-by-two grid.
- The colour sheet turns its columns into four stacked rows. Each row's height keeps the 1.6 : 1 : 0.8 : 0.6 weighting with the hour on the left and readings on the right, so "each later hour gets less room" survives.
- The deep violet row's minimum height is whatever its readings need.
- The running head drops the sheet name and keeps time and page number, so the clock is never what gets cut.

**Everywhere:**
- Sheets that are full height use the stable small-viewport height, not the legacy viewport height.
- Nothing scrolls sideways. Sheets already clip horizontally, so a numeral that would overflow is a sizing bug, not something to hide.
- The ember canvas is unchanged: its field size is already orientation-aware, it already reallocates on resize, and it is already capped for phones.

**Print:**
- The numerals print at the same proportion of the page, which puts the spread numeral at about 250 pt on A4 landscape.
- Running-head times print.

## 9. What it must not do

- **No images outside the guide's current set.** No board images, and no new images of any kind. Each image is used once in the document.
- **No imagery of Black women with short or shaved hair.**
- **No sheet lighter than the sheet before it.** Grounds keep their place in the fall.
- **The hours stay as set.** They are never changed, reordered or used decoratively (no clock faces, no ticking, no animation of the numerals). They are static type.
- **No scrim, shadow or blend on numerals over photographs.**
- **No widening of the numerals.** 150% width is the wordmark's.
- **Nothing dropped.** No reading (hex, OKLCH, CMYK, contrast) is removed from a sheet, and no page number is removed.
- **No B-style climbing names**, and no A-style horizon.
- **No change to the ember's behaviour.**
- **No change to the Shopify theme.** That is Task 5, still deferred.
